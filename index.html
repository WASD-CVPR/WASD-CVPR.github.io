    Results · LIBERO-Recover

    Every episode starts from a failure.

    Zero-shot gains on two WAMs

    +14.8 pts on OpenWAM, on par with an oracle that reads the simulator.

    Success against agent-heavy systems

    43× fewer tokens than an agent harness: 30.2k vs. 1.30M per episode.

    Post-training and guidance add up

    71.8 % with WASD-Tune. Naive post-training drops to 50.1 %.

    When should the agent step in?

    Start + failures gets most of the gain. Denser schedules stop the robot far more often.

    What each part is worth

    Replacing the written frames with text costs the most: −15.3 pts.

    Standard LIBERO skills are kept

    Interpolating back toward the released weights keeps what the WAM already does well.
